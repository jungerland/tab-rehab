# Surface brief: tab list (tab_rehab.py)

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show far more tabs on one screen than a typical list tool, so a purge reads as one scan. Refuses the airy single-column list with big padded rows.

OWN-WORLD: Japanese high-density portal grammar. White ground, near-black ink, cool gray hairline rules and panel fills, one utility red (#d7000f). Compact gothic sans (Hiragino Sans, then system). Red means two related things: a window's identity tab, and the act of purging (close). Site monograms use muted per-host hues and stay small.

STORY: Jay sees every window at once, scans one-line rows by title and site, closes what goes, and moves keepers by dropping them on a window or its chip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and total at top left. A sticky gray bar holds the filter (labeled), the "N of M" count, a red-tabbed chip per window (click to jump, drop to move), and Refresh at the right. Below it is a one-line hint. Window modules tile in a grid (min 460px), each ruled in hairlines and headed by a red letter tab, the window name, its count, and its top two domains. Rows are 30px: grip, monogram, title (ellipsis), host, then actions. View and copy show on hover or focus. Trash is always visible.

FORM: chosen challenger "High-Density Grid" (catalog japanese-high-density-web), over the assigned Garage Sale Dots. Seed key 9399ed3b. Signature move: the red letter tab names a window everywhere (module header, bar chips, and later the Move-to menu and undo tray).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
